## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. Clarix — AI-Powered Legal Intelligence Platform

A MERN-based legal intelligence platform designed to simplify contract analysis and risk assessment.

- Automated contract risk scoring.
- Secure authentication using JWT and bcrypt.
- Rate limiting and Jest unit testing.
- LangChain-powered RAG assistant for legal queries.

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-FF4380?style=flat-square&logo=vercel&logoColor=white)](https://clarix-psi.vercel.app/)
[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/nehalikareddy/Clarix)

### 2. Unshell — Autonomous AML & KYB Graph

🏆 **2nd Runner-Up — National HackFest**

An autonomous financial investigation platform that streamlines Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Business (KYB) workflows.

- React frontend with Node.js and Express backend.
- Python-based LangGraph processing pipeline.
- DAG cycle detection and sanctions screening.
- Reduced investigation time from 2–3 days to under 10 seconds.

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-FF4380?style=flat-square&logo=vercel&logoColor=white)](https://unshell-eight.vercel.app/)
[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/nehalikareddy/unshell)

### 3. TheCarePal (MedSync) — Real-Time Medication & Caregiver Platform

A full-stack healthcare coordination platform designed to improve medication adherence and caregiver communication.

- Medication scheduling and adherence analytics.
- Real-time caregiver coordination using Socket.IO.
- React and Vite frontend.
- Node.js backend with MongoDB.

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-FF4380?style=flat-square&logo=vercel&logoColor=white)](https://the-care-pal-amr8.vercel.app/)
